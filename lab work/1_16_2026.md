## Results

Today, me and Dmytro went to the lab to assemble a version of the circuit for firmware development.

- Assembled a circuit consisting of an MSP evaluation board and our AFE chip on an empty PCB, with the two chips connected through jumper wires, with SPI lines connected to an oscilloscope and power supplied from a lab DC source
- We were able to output correct numbers through SPI, but we learned the SPI driver pulls CS high between each transaction, which is not acceptable for our AFE chip.
- Because of that, nothing was showing up on MISO.
- Next steps: adjust firmware so we can communicate with the AFE and read back data.
