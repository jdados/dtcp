# 01/18/2026

Today, we met as a group in the lab to continue working on the firmware and the power amplifier.
We started working at 3pm and ended at 6pm.

For the firmware side (Dmytro and Jerimiah), the results were:

- We made the CS a GPIO pin in order to have more control over it, specifically in order to have valid SPI transactions as defined by the datasheet for the chip, and now can properly pull down and pull up the pin.
- We were able to get 0.034 A (instead of 0.01 ~ 0 A initially) to be output by the chip, indicating that the chip is actually powered up.
- With the current code, we also get data from MISO and can now properly read from registers (for now, only tested with reset values).

For the hardware side (Luis and Jerimiah):

- We constructed a test circuit on a separate board for the class-E amplifier.
- Our current goal as we wait for the pre-built PA chip to arrive is to confirm the design outputs at close to 30dBm.
- We made use of parts on hand to approximate the values for the capicitors and inductors used in simuluation.
  - Drain inductor used was 1.5 uH.
  - Approximated 120 pF to 112pF using two 56pF capacitors in parallel.
  - Approximated a 120nH and 85nH in series with two 68nH and two 39nH inductors in series.
