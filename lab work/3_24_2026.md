## Results

### Firmware (Jeremiah, Luis, Dmytro)

- Binary UART code allows computer to recieve data without the spikes present before.
    - Encountered syncing issues that could be resolved with header bits.
- Talked with our PHD student Han Wu in order to generate a test signal that more accurately reflects a real signal.
    - This signal has a large artifact in the beginning (an EEG signal)
    - Followed by the EMG signal we wish to record.
- We will use this signal to create an algorithm that detects the artifact and filters it out.
    - Presumably we just need to detect if the voltage meets a certain threshold and then add a delay before starting the recording once this artifact is detected.

- Increased the EMG sampling rate to 8kS/s and UART baud rate to 800 kbaud to enable the capture of brief EMG transients.

- Determined that the previous sampling rate of 1 kHz was not enough for our purposes
- After increasing the sampling rate to the 16 kS/s (maximum), we encountered spiking and found out that UART baud rate of 115,200 bps could only transmit 3.6 kS/s
- After testing different configurations of the sampling rates and baud rates, it seems that the fastest we can do without any spiking is 8kS/s and 800 kbps
